Being Okay with Being Alone

May 10, 2025

"So how do you do it, Sasha? How do you go everywhere alone?" - my friend, one random morning.

Is it really that weird, though?

See, I didn't think I was weird, until people started asking me how I do it, and my mother began asking me who I'm going to places with, which means she assumes that.. yes, for this activity, you'd usually have friends with you.

I go everywhere alone. I shop, watch movies, watch the sun set, grocery shop, sit in restaurants, eat alone. I didn't think it was weird, I thought everyone did everything alone too, and going out with friends are, like, a luxury. Something you do every few weeks or so. It turns out, people bring their friends quite routinely.

At first, you see, I didn't think much of it. Okay, sure. I'm more of the alone type and that's okay, but along the way, it began to slip my lips.

"I just wish I had someone to go with me."

I mean, even I was surprised when I said that. When had I stopped enjoying my abundant me-time? When did I start craving for someone else to want to leave their home, their cozy, cozy homes to go do something with me? When have I started wanting to talk to someone after movies, during meals, and as the sun sets? So.. mushy, right? Ew.

But God help me, I meant it. I meant it so much. The more I go out alone these days, the more I crave people. I crave being around others. I have friend groups - multiple, even. But I feel lonely still. And I don't even know why.

Am I okay with being alone? Yes. Am I happy about it? Sasha 2 years ago would've said absolutely, yes, no doubt, mhm, of course. I'm afraid I have changed.
