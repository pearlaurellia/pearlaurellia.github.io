3D Modeling & Animating

May 10, 2025

Started learning 3D modeling a year ago. Today, I'm the coordinator for the 2D/3D division for an organization.

Meant to Be?

I started 3D modeling because I simply wanted to, and thought, yeah, surely I can do that, right? And I guess I was right?

How does one 3D model? It might seem daunting at first, but as soon as you get the logic down, and you can connect one point to another, you're all set. Unless you want to make professional models, render for hours only for a 10 second render, you really don't need much to learn 3D modeling as a hobby. My first creation was a teacup. Then the rite of passage donut. Then now, this windowsill that you see, that I eventually animated.

I am now.. teaching?

Honestly, I didn't see this one coming when I first started 3D modeling, because what do you MEAN I'm about to start teaching people? So weird. I feel like I barely know 3D modeling & animating at all.
